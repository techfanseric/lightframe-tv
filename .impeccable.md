## Design Context

### Users
The owner uses a Xiaomi Android TV with a D-pad remote to watch existing video apps. Lightframe stays above the playing app as a temporary overlay; automatic startup and playback continuity matter more than a conventional home screen.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, translucent, precise. The requested reference is Apple TV: confident focus, rounded rectangular application tiles, uncluttered hierarchy.

### Aesthetic Direction
One bottom row, generous but television-sized targets, local dark control scrims and a subtle scale-only focus. Preserve most of the picture without global dimming. Brand-colored, label-free tiles. YouTube retains TizenTube attribution in its accessibility description. Settings and smart-home controls appear above only while that category or its controls have focus. Native Android typography supports legible Chinese from the sofa; use the platform font to match the existing product and avoid unnecessary font payloads.

### Design Principles
1. Video remains the main surface; no replacement home activity or wallpaper.
2. Preserve startup, MRU, current-app exclusion, network checks and playback guardian.
3. Focus reveals controls, but never launches an app or changes a device setting.
4. Left/right wrap; up enters controls; down returns to their category; back closes the overlay.
5. Keep controls readable with local translucent surfaces. The clock matches the transparent Clash strip using fine glyph outlines; entry tiles use solid brand colors and no visible labels.

Final TV tuning: bottom scrim, focus scale 1.12; fixed-height category region prevents dock motion; IPTV flat screen glyph and smart-home connected nodes. Clock is transparent 9sp regular with the same outline, 18dp height and top alignment as Clash.

Settings/home panels: four compact columns, 46dp controls, content-height surfaces anchored above dock; no borders. Phosphor Fill for all panel content icons. ADB entry opens the system switch page.
